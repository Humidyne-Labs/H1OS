# Mild, Unadulterated, Unsolicited, Praise, YAY! 🤩

- Dev kit employs the use of **REAL** metal standoffs! (NO mechanical fasteners in plastic) \o/ yay!  
- The case separates into two, clean, independent, bodies! \o/ YAY!
- Audio playback is more than audible at a 40% reduction of `perceived volume unit placeholder`. \o/ YAY!!!!
- Form factor is adequate. \o/ YAYYY!!!!!!
- Screen legibility exceeded my expectations for an EPD. \o/ YAY! 
- Mysteriously small lithium polymer battery, sustains the device for a duration of time. Said duration is dependent on the devices average current draw during runtime. \o/ yay!!!!
- Device features a standard USB type C connector for programming and charging. \o/ Yay!!
- Two physical clicky buttons!!! boot0 is actually a button you can access without complete disassembley! \o/ yay!!!!
- Micro processor (SOC) has a risc-v coprocessor, that you can actually use! \o/ yayay!!
- The device has a **HUGE** memory capacity! for large application binaries! \o/ yay!!!!
- There's a charge indicator light, and auxiliary LED, that supports PWM via periferal! \o/ yay!!!
- All onboard chips either have a deep sleep mode or supper low quiescent current draw! For hypothetical extended runtime on the mysteriously small, 400mah lipo pack. \o/ yay!!!
- The SHTC3 is factory calibrated and NIST traceable! \o/ yay!!
- The case material (suspecting injection molding) is made from a polycarbonate material (composition unknown, no material datasheet provided), which "CAN" be classified as food safe due to its chemical stability (very little off gassing, little to no permeability, etc...). \o/ yay!!
- EPD is available in 3-color mode & with an integrated touch overlay/controller. Options! \o/ yay!!!! (no black/pink/blue/red polycarb case option) but still... yay!
- The manufacturer provides a cad file, which I haven't actually inspected my self, assumed to a valid step model. yay!! assumptions!! \o/
- Real external RTC with **REAL** Xtal! and configurable interrupt pin, actually connected to the mcu! RTC is or-ed with the raw vbat rail! for zero instability across power cycles! \o/ yay!! rock solid chronography!!
- Most of the components are pre-certified as ROHS compliant. \o/ yay!!!
- The SOC support BLE & WIFI! **NATIVELY**! \o/ yay!!
