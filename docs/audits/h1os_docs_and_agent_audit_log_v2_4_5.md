## 1. Executive Summary

Following the architectural transition to **H1OS `v2.4.5-dev`** (`MANDATE-02`, `MANDATE-04`, `MANDATE-06`, and codebase audit remediations `REV-01` through `REV-08`), a complete line-by-line audit was conducted across the documentation and agent governance archive (`h1os_repo_docs_arch.zip`).

The audit identified and resolved **18 distinct architectural, technical, and agent-governance discrepancies** (`DOC-01` through `DOC-11` and `AGENT-01` through `AGENT-07`) spanning **36 files**:

1. **Synchronization with `v2.4.5-dev` Dual-Core Concurrency & Safety Architecture:** Updated all lifecycle, orchestrator, state-machine, and public developer guides to document the parallel Core 0 `on_sync` hook, the static FreeRTOS `EventGroup` sleep barrier (`BARRIER_SYNC_DONE | BARRIER_DISPLAY_DONE`), the 16-slot static `h1os_ui_post()` cross-core actor queue drained inside `h1os_lvgl_lock(100)`, the `session.sync_wait_ms` single-refresh inbound coalescing gate, explicit `h1os_wake_state_t` classification, monotonic `flow_b_counter` / `button_wake_count` counters (`648-byte` `orch_persisted_state_t`), and the `MANDATE-06` fatal startup battery latch cut (`on_error` + `h1os_handle_fatal_startup_error()`).
2. **Elimination of Stale & Contradictory Hardware Claims:** Corrected a critical hardware contradiction in `C1_benchmarks_and_verification.md` that erroneously instructed holding `PA_EN` (`GPIO 42`) HIGH and writing `Reg 0x0D = 0x00` (which violates Errata E4 and clamps the shared I2C bus), removed references to the retired `sub_sensor` Layer 2 module and retired `h1os_session_monitor_task` polling task, updated the cold-boot flow-period initialization split (`h1os_validate_flow_periods()` + `h1os_apply_flow_periods()`), and documented the mandatory `250 ms` post-`BUSY` electrophoretic settling delay inside `sub_display_enter_deep_sleep()` (`REV-04`).
3. **Embedded C/C++ Tuning of `AGENTS.md`, `.agents/rules/`, and `.agents/skills/`:** Tuned `esp-idf-codegen/SKILL.md` to mandate atomic `.h` + `.c`/`.cpp` edits (replacing a single-file restriction that broke C header/source compilation coherency), fixed `h1os_lvgl_lock(uint32_t timeout_ms)` signatures and Core 1 priority (`5`) in `lvgl.md` and `lvgl-ui/SKILL.md`, updated all build/clangd paths from legacy `examples/H1OS` to `examples/H1OS`, removed a prohibited lexicon term (`"Sentinel"`) and wrong magic constant (`0x48314F53` $\rightarrow$ `0x534C4154` `'SLAT'`) in `doxygen.md`, and expanded `h1os-firmware-standards/SKILL.md` and `B3_firmware_standards.md` from 11 to 14 embedded safety invariants.
