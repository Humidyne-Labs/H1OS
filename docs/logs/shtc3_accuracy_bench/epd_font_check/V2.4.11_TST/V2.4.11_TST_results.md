# H1OS v2.4.10 — Empirical Visual & Typographic Legibility Audit (`TST_results`)

**Target Firmware:** `H1OS v2.4.10` (`IDF v6.1.1`)  
**Display Module:** Waveshare 1.54-inch e-Paper V2 (`200 × 200` px, `188 DPI`, hard-coat antiglare surface, pure reflective mode)  
**Display Driver IC:** Solomon Systech `SSD1681` (`200` source × `200` gate outputs, 1-bit B/W display RAM)  
**Physical Display Geometry:** `27.00 mm × 27.00 mm` active area; **`0.135 mm × 0.135 mm` (`135 μm`)** square pixel pitch  
**Measured Viewing Distance:** **`200 mm`** (`20 cm` eye-to-EPD)  
**Empirical Reference Targets (`200 mm` Viewing Distance):**
* **20/20 Snellen Minimum Angle of Resolution (`1.0 arcmin`):** `0.058 mm` (`58 μm` / `0.43 EPD px`)
* **Single EPD Pixel Angular Subtense (`135 μm`):** `2.32 arcmin`
* **Optimal Minimum Stroke Width:** `≥ 203–270 μm` (`≥ 1.5–2.0 EPD px`) to exceed the `169 μm` (`1.25 EPD px`) antiglare + microcapsule scattering radius
* **Optimal Minimum Interior Counter Opening:** `≥ 270 μm` (`≥ 2.0 EPD px`) to prevent optical aperture closure at `200 mm`
* **Optimal Panel Contrast Ratio (CR):** `≥ 7.0:1` (`Michelson Contrast C_M ≥ 0.750`)
* **Optimal Gaussian PSF Contrast Retention (`σ = 169 μm` / `1.25 EPD px`):** `≥ 45.0%`

## 1. Screen-Level Empirical Photometric & Contrast Results

### 1.1 Screen 1 — `HUMIDYNE LABS` (`1/5`)
* **Metric:** Mid-panel background reflectance `190.0` (8-bit greyscale), ink density `23.0`, Contrast Ratio `8.26:1`, Michelson Contrast `C_M = 0.784`.
* **Comparison:** Above the optimal `≥ 7.0:1` CR (`+18.0%`) and `C_M ≥ 0.750` baseline for the `188 DPI` reflective EPD.
* **Conclusion:** **PASS** (Global Panel Contrast). Best overall panel contrast across all five screens, though localized `135 μm` (`1.0 EPD px`) glyph strokes fail local retention targets (see Sections 2 & 3).

### 1.2 Screen 2 — `PSYCHROMETRICS` (`2/5`)
* **Metric:** Mid-panel background reflectance `194.0` (8-bit greyscale), ink density `29.0`, Contrast Ratio `6.69:1`, Michelson Contrast `C_M = 0.740`.
* **Comparison:** Slightly below the optimal `≥ 7.0:1` CR (`-4.4%`) and `C_M ≥ 0.750` target, with measurable residual ghosting from Screen 1 (`SETTLED` / `13.3 hPa`) in the central whitespace.
* **Conclusion:** **MARGINAL PASS** (Global Panel Contrast). Broadly legible at `200 mm`, except for `135 μm` curved stems and the `0.54 × 0.68 mm` (`4 × 5 px`) superscript `³` in `g/m³`.

### 1.3 Screen 3 — `THERMAL NODES` (`3/5`)
* **Metric:** Mid-panel background reflectance `193.0` (8-bit greyscale), ink density `31.0`, Contrast Ratio `6.23:1`, Michelson Contrast `C_M = 0.723`.
* **Comparison:** Below the optimal `≥ 7.0:1` CR (`-11.0%`) and `C_M ≥ 0.750` target due to elevated ink greyscale floor (`31.0` vs. `23.0` on Screen 1).
* **Conclusion:** **MARGINAL** (Global Panel Contrast). Row labels and temperature values remain legible at `200 mm`, with localized `135 μm` thinning on the left diagonal of `Δ` (`TriNode ΔT`) and lowercase `s` (`Glass`, `Stability`).

### 1.4 Screen 4 — `SYSTEM HEALTH` (`4/5`)
* **Metric:** Mid-panel background reflectance `184.0` (8-bit greyscale), ink density `35.0`, Contrast Ratio `5.26:1`, Michelson Contrast `C_M = 0.680`.
* **Comparison:** Substantially below the optimal `≥ 7.0:1` CR (`-24.9%`) and `C_M ≥ 0.750` target, recording both the darkest background floor and lightest ink density across the capture set.
* **Conclusion:** **FAIL** (Global Panel Contrast Target). While uppercase values (`BATTERY`, `CALIBRATE`, `NO CARD`, `ACTIVE`) remain readable, the reduced contrast margin compounds `135 μm` stroke starvation on `Source` and `Autonomy`.

### 1.5 Screen 5 — `OS DIAGNOSTICS` (`5/5`)
* **Metric:** Mid-panel background reflectance `194.0` (8-bit greyscale), ink density `25.0`, Contrast Ratio `7.76:1`, Michelson Contrast `C_M = 0.772`.
* **Comparison:** Above the optimal `≥ 7.0:1` CR (`+10.9%`) and `C_M ≥ 0.750` baseline.
* **Conclusion:** **PASS** (Global Panel Contrast). High contrast is restored across all 9 text rows; uppercase telemetry is crisp, while lowercase `s` (`Reset`, `Wakes`) exhibits the same `135 μm` curve collapse seen on Screen 1.

## 2. Screen 1 String-Level Empirical Results (`200 mm` Viewing Distance)

### 2.1 Header String: `HUMIDYNE LABS`
* **Metric:** Glyph height `1.30 mm` (`9.6 EPD px`), mean stroke width `186 μm` (`1.38 EPD px`), minimum stroke width `120 μm` (`0.89 EPD px`), raw `C_M = 0.787`, Gaussian PSF contrast retention `35.7%` at `σ = 169 μm` (`27.9%` at `σ = 236 μm`).
* **Comparison:** Mean stroke width is `8.4%` below the optimal `≥ 203 μm` (`1.5 EPD px`) minimum stroke target, and `σ = 169 μm` contrast retention is `9.3 percentage points` below the `≥ 45.0%` target.
* **Conclusion:** **MARGINAL PASS**. Vertical linear stems (`H`, `U`, `M`, `I`, `D`, `N`, `E`, `L`, `B`) remain cleanly separated at `200 mm`, while `S` exhibits tight interior bowls.

### 2.2 Hero Telemetry Strings: `78.2°F` & `40.3%`
* **Metric:** Glyph height `2.47 mm` (`18.3 EPD px` including degree/percent symbols; `1.78 mm` / `13.2 EPD px` digit cap height), mean stroke width `371 μm` (`2.75 EPD px`), minimum stroke width `242 μm` (`1.79 EPD px`), raw `C_M = 0.853`, Gaussian PSF contrast retention `65.6%` at `σ = 169 μm` (`56.8%` at `σ = 236 μm`).
* **Comparison:** Mean stroke width exceeds the `≥ 270 μm` (`2.0 EPD px`) target by `+37.4%`, and `σ = 169 μm` contrast retention exceeds the `≥ 45.0%` target by `+20.6 percentage points`.
* **Conclusion:** **PASS**. Primary hero telemetry is clearly legible at `200 mm`, with localized waist softening confined to the digit `8` (see Section 3.4).

### 2.3 Sub-Hero String: `(25.7°C ambient)`
* **Metric:** Glyph height `1.71 mm` (`12.7 EPD px` parenthetical; `0.945 mm` / `7.0 EPD px` lowercase x-height), mean stroke width `161 μm` (`1.19 EPD px`), minimum stroke width `120 μm` (`0.89 EPD px`), raw `C_M = 0.784`, Gaussian PSF contrast retention `32.5%` at `σ = 169 μm` (`26.2%` at `σ = 236 μm`).
* **Comparison:** Mean stroke width is `20.7%` below the optimal `≥ 203 μm` (`1.5 EPD px`) minimum target, and `σ = 169 μm` contrast retention is `12.5 percentage points` below the `≥ 45.0%` threshold.
* **Conclusion:** **FAIL**. Curved lowercase bowls (`e`, `a`, `c`) and parentheses thin to `135 μm` (`1.0 EPD px`), reducing edge definition at `200 mm`.

### 2.4 Body Row 1 Strings: `Status` & `SETTLED (90%)`
* **Metric:** Glyph height `1.69 mm` (`12.5 EPD px` parenthetical; `0.945 mm` / `7.0 EPD px` x-height), mean stroke width `166 μm` (`1.23 EPD px`), minimum stroke width `120 μm` (`0.89 EPD px`), raw `C_M = 0.829`, Gaussian PSF contrast retention `38.2%` at `σ = 169 μm` (`32.2%` at `σ = 236 μm`).
* **Comparison:** Mean stroke width is `18.2%` below the `≥ 203 μm` minimum target, and `σ = 169 μm` contrast retention is `6.8 percentage points` below the `≥ 45.0%` threshold.
* **Conclusion:** **LOCALIZED FAIL**. While uppercase `SETTLED` remains readable via its vertical stems (`T`, `L`, `D`), the lowercase `s` characters in `Status` and the right-hand arcs of `9` and `0` in `(90%)` fail the `200 mm` legibility threshold.

### 2.5 Body Row 2 Strings: `Vapor Pressure` & `13.3 hPa`
* **Metric:** Glyph height `1.66 mm` (`12.3 EPD px`), mean stroke width `170 μm` (`1.26 EPD px`), minimum stroke width `120 μm` (`0.89 EPD px`), raw `C_M = 0.810`, Gaussian PSF contrast retention `36.2%` at `σ = 169 μm` (`30.2%` at `σ = 236 μm`).
* **Comparison:** Mean stroke width is `16.3%` below the `≥ 203 μm` minimum target, and `σ = 169 μm` contrast retention is `8.8 percentage points` below the `≥ 45.0%` threshold.
* **Conclusion:** **LOCALIZED FAIL**. The adjacent `ss` pair in `Pressure` forms a `3.55 cycles/mm` (`90.2 cycles/in`) periodic grating with `135 μm` bars and `135 μm` gaps that fuses into a continuous grey smear at `200 mm`.

### 2.6 Footer Strings: `Cycle #3` & `02:48 AM`
* **Metric:** Glyph height `1.66 mm` (`12.3 EPD px`), mean stroke width `151 μm` (`1.12 EPD px`), minimum stroke width `120 μm` (`0.89 EPD px`), raw `C_M = 0.775`, Gaussian PSF contrast retention `39.0%` at `σ = 169 μm` (`34.3%` at `σ = 236 μm`).
* **Comparison:** Mean stroke width is `25.6%` below the `≥ 203 μm` minimum target (lowest mean thickness on Screen 1), and `σ = 169 μm` contrast retention is `6.0 percentage points` below the `≥ 45.0%` threshold.
* **Conclusion:** **LOCALIZED FAIL**. Enclosed counters in `02:48` collapse under antiglare scattering, producing visual fuzziness across the timestamp digits.

## 3. Character-Level Empirical Impairment Results

### 3.1 Lowercase `s` (in `Status`, `Pressure`, `Glass`, `Reset`, `Wakes`)
* **Metric:** Bounding box `0.675 mm (W) × 0.945 mm (H)` (`5 × 7 EPD px`), horizontal bar and vertical curve stroke width `135 μm` (`1.0 EPD px`), interior upper/lower cavity dimensions `135 μm (H) × 405 μm (W)` (`1 × 3 EPD px`), raw local `C_M = 0.47` (single `s` in `Status`) and `C_M = 0.36` (`ss` pair in `Pressure`), Gaussian PSF contrast retention `35.2%` (`C_M = 0.23`) for single `s` and `32.4%` (`C_M = 0.14`) for `ss` at `σ = 169 μm` (falling to `19.7%–21.9%` at the stroke centerlines).
* **Comparison:** Stroke width (`135 μm`) is `50.0%` below the optimal `270 μm` (`2.0 EPD px`) target, interior cavity height (`135 μm`) is `50.0%` below the optimal `270 μm` counter opening, and post-PSF `C_M` (`0.14–0.23`) falls below the human contrast-sensitivity threshold (`~0.25`) at `12.6 cycles/deg` (`200 mm`).
* **Conclusion:** **CRITICAL FAIL**. Lowercase `s` is objectively unreadable at `200 mm`; its internal apertures close completely and adjacent `ss` glyphs (`Pressure`) alias into an undifferentiated smudge.

### 3.2 Body Digit `8` (in `02:48`)
* **Metric:** Bounding box `0.810 mm (W) × 1.350 mm (H)` (`6 × 10 EPD px`), left vertical stem `270 μm` (`2.0 EPD px`), right vertical stem `135 μm` (`1.0 EPD px`), middle crossbar `135 μm` (`1.0 EPD px`), upper and lower enclosed counters `135 μm (W) × 270 μm (H)` (`1 × 2 EPD px`), Gaussian PSF contrast retention `26.3%` at the waist (`C_M = 0.36` overall at `σ = 169 μm`).
* **Comparison:** Right stem width (`135 μm`) and horizontal counter width (`135 μm`) are both `50.0%` below the optimal `270 μm` (`2.0 EPD px`) minimum specification for the `188 DPI` panel.
* **Conclusion:** **FAIL**. Both interior counters fill in under `σ = 169 μm` antiglare diffusion, rendering `8` fuzzy and ambiguous against `B` or `0` at `200 mm`.

### 3.3 Body Digits `2`, `0`, `3`, `9` (in `02:48`, `13.3`, `(90%)`)
* **Metric:** Bounding box `0.675–0.810 mm (W) × 1.350 mm (H)` (`5–6 × 10 EPD px`), minimum diagonal/right-arc stroke width `135 μm` (`1.0 EPD px`), Gaussian PSF contrast retention `25.1%–27.8%` along the `135 μm` right-hand arcs (`0`, `9`) and diagonal spine (`2`) at `σ = 169 μm`.
* **Comparison:** Single-pixel (`135 μm`) right-hand stems and diagonal steps are `33.5%–50.0%` below the optimal `203–270 μm` minimum stroke width, and `17.2–19.9 percentage points` below the `≥ 45.0%` contrast retention target.
* **Conclusion:** **FAIL**. Produces asymmetric right-edge fade on `0` and `9`, midsection breakup on `2`, and aperture pinching on `3` at `200 mm`.

### 3.4 Hero Digit `8` (in `78.2°F`)
* **Metric:** Bounding box `1.350 mm (W) × 1.782 mm (H)` (`10 × 13.2 EPD px`), mean stroke width `351 μm` (`2.60 EPD px`), minimum waist/crossbar thickness `217 μm` (`1.61 EPD px`), enclosed counters `270 μm × 270 μm` (`2 × 2 EPD px`), Gaussian PSF contrast retention `44.4%` at `σ = 169 μm`.
* **Comparison:** Contrast retention (`44.4%`) is `0.6 percentage points` below the `≥ 45.0%` target and `11.9–18.2 percentage points` lower than neighboring hero glyphs `7` (`56.3%`), `2` (`56.8%`), and `F` (`62.6%`).
* **Conclusion:** **MARGINAL**. Legible at `200 mm`, but the compressed `270 × 270 μm` counters and `217 μm` waist create localized fuzziness relative to adjacent hero characters.

### 3.5 Uppercase `S` (in `HUMIDYNE LABS`, `SETTLED`, `H10S`) and `0`/`O` Pair (`H10S` vs. `SHT:OK`)
* **Metric:** Bounding box `0.810–0.945 mm (W) × 1.215–1.350 mm (H)` (`6–7 × 9–10 EPD px`), spine/curve thickness `135–189 μm` (`1.0–1.4 EPD px`), interior counter height `270 μm` (`2.0 EPD px`), Gaussian PSF contrast retention `21.9%–30.4%` at `σ = 169 μm`. On Screen 5, `0` (`H10S`) and `O` (`SHT:OK`) share identical `0.810 mm` (`6 px`) widths and `189 μm` (`1.4 px`) stroke profiles with no slash or dot differentiator.
* **Comparison:** Curve thickness (`135–189 μm`) falls `6.9%–33.5%` below the `≥ 203 μm` minimum target, and `σ = 169 μm` contrast retention is `14.6–23.1 percentage points` below the `≥ 45.0%` target.
* **Conclusion:** **MARGINAL**. More legible than lowercase `s` due to its `270 μm` (`2 px`) counter height, but exhibits curve softening and `0`/`O` character ambiguity (`H10S` vs. `H1OS`).
